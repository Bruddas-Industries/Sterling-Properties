Introducing The Arden

Natural beauty, timeless sophistication.

The Arden is a new community from Sterling Properties, taking shape at Sterling Pointe in Franklin Township, New Jersey.

Its name draws on the scenic landscapes that surround it, from the wooded trails of Six Mile Run to the gardens and open green spaces of Colonial Park, paired with a refined, contemporary spirit made for modern living.

The Setting

Rooted in Franklin Township.

The Arden is surrounded by some of central New Jersey's most scenic landscapes: wooded trails, historic canal paths and acres of gardens and open green space.

Six Mile Run

Wooded trails, streams and quiet farmland in Six Mile Run State Park, part of Delaware & Raritan Canal State Park.

Colonial Park

Somerset County's park of lawns, gardens and open green space, home to the Rudolf W. van der Goot Rose Garden.

The D&R Canal

The historic Delaware & Raritan Canal towpath, for walking, running and cycling along the Millstone River.
