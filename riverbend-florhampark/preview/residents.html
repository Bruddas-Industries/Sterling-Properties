Community Guidelines

Living at Riverbend

A brief overview of key community services. Full details are in your lease agreement and community rules document.

Garages & Parking
  • — Private garages with remote electric door openers
  • — Electric-vehicle charging on site
  • — Ask the leasing office about guest parking
Pets
  • — Cats and small dogs are welcome in select buildings
  • — Ask the leasing office for the full pet policy
Chatham Station Shuttle
  • — Complimentary courtesy shuttle on weekdays
  • — Morning and evening service to the Chatham train station
  • — The leasing office has the current schedule
Maintenance
  • — Submit requests through the resident portal
  • — Emergency maintenance available 24/7
  • — Report issues promptly so the onsite team can help
Grounds & Snow Removal
  • — Professionally landscaped grounds
  • — Snow removal in winter
Clubhouse & Pool
  • — Renovated clubhouse lounge and fitness center for residents
  • — Outdoor pool, sun patio and BBQ area in season
  • — Ask the leasing office for current hours
