Welcome to Bellclair

A Boutique Address on Bell Street

Bellclair is a boutique community by Sterling Properties, set on a quiet street at the edge of downtown Montclair. Spacious one- and two-bedroom residences pair modern kitchens and in-unit laundry with a secured lobby, a fitness center and garage parking — all a short walk from the town's restaurants, museums and shops.

Community Amenities

Everyday Comforts, Close to Home

Select an amenity for a quick detail.

A state-of-the-art fitness center with cardio machines and free weights — no gym membership required.

An elegant foyer with secured entrances, so only residents and their guests come and go.

Underground garage parking and on-site reserved spaces, with private garages at select townhome residences.

Our leasing and management team works on site at 691 Bloomfield Avenue, steps from the residences.

ChargePoint electric-vehicle charging on site, so you can charge at home.

Cats are welcome at Bellclair. Dogs are not permitted.

Our on-site maintenance team is available 24/7. Submit a request online any time, or call for emergencies around the clock.
